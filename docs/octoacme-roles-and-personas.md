# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Business Analysts

### Role Summary
Business Analysts translate business needs into clear requirements, workflows, and acceptance details. They help teams align on scope, assumptions, and process impacts before work is delivered.

### Responsibilities
- Document business processes, requirements, and decision points
- Clarify requirements, dependencies, and edge cases with Product Managers and Project Managers
- Support backlog refinement with detailed acceptance considerations
- Help ensure changes are traceable to business needs and expected outcomes

### Goals
- Reduce ambiguity in requirements and handoffs
- Improve shared understanding across business and delivery teams
- Increase the likelihood that delivered work solves the intended problem

### Typical Communication
- Requirements workshops and discovery sessions
- Backlog refinement notes and process walkthroughs
- Follow-up clarification in tickets, comments, and shared documents

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Partner with Project Managers to identify dependencies, assumptions, and timeline risks
- Work with Product Managers to refine problem statements, scope, and acceptance criteria
- Support Developers with requirement clarification, workflows, and business rules during implementation
- Collaborate with QA/Testing to make sure scenarios, edge cases, and validation steps are understood
- Help Stakeholders review proposed changes and confirm that the solution matches business expectations

---

## Release Managers

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate release readiness across teams so changes move to production in a controlled, well-communicated way. They help ensure deployment timing, approvals, and rollback planning are clear.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain release plans, checklists, and readiness criteria
- Coordinate release dependencies, approvals, and go/no-go decisions
- Track operational risks, rollback plans, and release communications
- Confirm that documentation, validation, and handoff activities are complete before release

### Goals
- Improve release predictability and reduce deployment risk
- Ensure teams are prepared for launch, support, and recovery if issues arise
- Create clear accountability for production readiness

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews and launch coordination meetings
- Deployment schedules, release notes, and incident communication channels
- Status updates before, during, and after production releases

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Coordinate with Project Managers on milestones, risk tracking, and cross-team scheduling
- Align with Product Managers on launch scope, timing, and customer-impact communication
- Work with Developers to confirm deployment steps, technical dependencies, and rollback readiness
- Partner with QA/Testing to verify test completion, defect status, and release sign-off inputs
- Keep Stakeholders informed about release timing, readiness, and any launch risks or changes
